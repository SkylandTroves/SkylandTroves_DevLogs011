Title: Skyland Troves
Authors: Aliyah Rafei, Cassie Thanos, Lauren Sun, Diego Murillo
Course: GAME 440-01

Game Instructions: Solve the puzzles by charging up orbs and then dropping them on the appropriate podiums. Turn wheels to raise/lower platforms and interact with other moveable platforms.

Controls: Left click to move. Right click to drop orbs. Scroll to turn wheels.

Known bugs: There might be a potential perfomance drop in frames when spamming left click to move.
